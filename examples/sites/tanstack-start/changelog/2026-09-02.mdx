title: "Tickets API"
date: 2026-09-02
version: "3.1.0"
description: "Create, update and close tickets from your own app."
tags: [Feature]
---

- `POST /v1/tickets` creates a ticket with the customer's email, subject, body and metadata
- `PATCH /v1/tickets/:id` changes status, assignee and tags
- Ticket metadata shows in the sidebar and works as a view filter
- **Test** API keys that never reach your team's queue

See the [guide](/blog/tickets-api) for examples.
