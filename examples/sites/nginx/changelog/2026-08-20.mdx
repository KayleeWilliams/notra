- **Object Lock** for S3, MinIO and Backblaze B2 targets, in `compliance` or `governance` mode
- `sb target add --retention` sets how long uploaded chunks stay locked
- Pruning skips locked chunks and retries them after the lock ends

Read the [setup guide](/blog/s3-object-lock-guide).
