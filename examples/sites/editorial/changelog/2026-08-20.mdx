- **Catchments:** right-click a stop and choose **Show walking area** to draw the area reachable on foot in 5, 10 or 15 minutes, using the street network and crossings
- Gradients slow the walk down, so catchments on hills come out smaller
- Fixed PDF exports dropping station names set in a custom font
- Fixed bundled lines drifting apart by one pixel at 400% zoom
