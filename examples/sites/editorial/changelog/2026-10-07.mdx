- **Insets:** select stations on the diagram and choose **Add inset** to place a true-scale street map of that area beside it
- Walking links between platforms get their own line style with a time label, like `6 min`
- Insets follow the map's palette and type settings, and export with the rest of the poster

Read why we built it in [The diagram and the street](/blog/the-diagram-and-the-street).
