Two small releases since v2.5.0. Upgrading to v2.5.2 picks up both.

<Update label="v2.5.2" description="September 16, 2026">
  Fixed date inputs saving the previous day for users west of UTC.

  `forge logs` now shows the action name instead of its internal id.
</Update>

<Update label="v2.5.1" description="September 9, 2026">
  Fixed actions retrying after a validation error. Only network errors and 5xx responses retry now.

  `defineAction` accepts `retries: 0` to turn retries off for one action.
</Update>
