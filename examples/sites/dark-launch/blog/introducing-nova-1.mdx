Nova joins your calls, writes the notes and sends everyone the action items before you've closed the tab. Today it leaves beta.

This is day 1 of launch week. Tomorrow we ship search across every meeting you've had. On Wednesday, shared workspaces and a public API.

## What Nova does

Invite `nova@meet.nova.ai` to a Zoom, Google Meet or Teams call, or let Nova join everything on your calendar. When the call ends, you get:

- a summary of five bullets or fewer, written for someone who wasn't there
- decisions, each with the timestamp where it was made
- action items with an owner, pulled from phrases like "I'll send that over"
- the full transcript, with speakers labeled by name

## What changed since the beta

The beta wrote good notes for English calls with up to four people. 1.0 is built for the calls people actually have.

| | Beta | 1.0 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Languages | English | 19, including mixed-language calls |
| Speakers told apart | Up to 4 | Up to 16 |
| Notes ready after the call | About 3 minutes | Under 30 seconds |
| Word error rate (our test set) | 11.2% | 6.8% |

<Note>
  Nova never trains on your meetings. Recordings are deleted after 30 days by default, and you can set that to zero.
</Note>

## Pricing

Nova is free for 10 meetings a month. **Pro** is $12 per person per month with no meeting limit. Everyone who joined the beta gets Pro free until the end of the year.
