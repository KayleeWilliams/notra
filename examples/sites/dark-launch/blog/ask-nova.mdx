"What did we promise Halden about the export?" Until today, answering that meant scrolling through three transcripts. Now you ask Nova.

## How it works

Ask opens with `⌘K` anywhere in Nova. Type a question, and Nova searches every meeting you were in, then answers in a few sentences. Each claim links to the moment in the recording, so you can hear it yourself.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ask in plain language">
    "When did we agree to move the launch?" works. So does "pricing objections from last week".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the sources">
    Every answer lists the meetings it used. Click a quote to jump to that second of the recording.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share the answer">
    Copy it as a link. Teammates who weren't in the meeting see the answer and the quotes, not the full call.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Questions people ask most

During the beta we watched which questions came up again and again. These four make up over half of all searches:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Sales">
    "What did the customer say about pricing?" Nova pulls every pricing mention from calls with that company, oldest first.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Product">
    "Which customers asked for SSO?" Nova lists them, with the date and a quote from each.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Managers">
    "What did I commit to this week?" Nova collects your action items from every call since Monday.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Privacy

Ask only searches meetings you were invited to or that someone shared with you. It doesn't see private meetings of other people in your workspace, even admins'.
