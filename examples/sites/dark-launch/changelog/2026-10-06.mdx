- Nova leaves beta, with a free plan for 10 meetings a month and **Pro** at $12 per person
- **Ask**: search every meeting in plain language, with answers linked to the second
- **Shared workspaces** with per-team note templates
- **Public API** and `meeting.notes_ready` webhooks
- Word error rate down from 11.2% to 6.8% on our test set
