In July we added a short survey to the waitlist email that Tidepool sends for 38 swim schools. 4,112 parents answered. Here is what they told us, and what we changed because of it.

## Waiting is fine. Silence isn't.

We expected the length of the wait to be the main complaint. It wasn't.

| Answer | Share of parents |
| --- | --- |
| "I'd wait up to a term for the right class" | 64% |
| "I don't know where we are on the list" | 58% |
| "I joined another school's waitlist too" | 41% |
| "The wait itself is the problem" | 17% |

More than half didn't know their place in the queue, and four in ten had hedged by signing up somewhere else. For a school, that second number is the expensive one. When a spot opens, the parent you call has often already enrolled down the road.

## What we changed

Waitlisted parents now get a short update every two weeks with their position for each class they want, and an estimate based on how fast that class has moved in past terms.

<CodeGroup>
```text Email
Hi Sam,

Maya is 3rd on the waitlist for Level 1, Saturdays 9:30.
Last term, 4 spots opened in this class.

We'll write again on 9 September.
```

```text SMS
Tidepool: Maya is 3rd for Level 1 (Sat 9:30). Next update 9 Sep.
```
</CodeGroup>

## Early results

Across the first six schools, the share of parents who accept a spot when offered went from 52% to 74%. The schools didn't open a single extra class. They just stopped losing families who had assumed they'd been forgotten.

<Info>
  The position update is on for every school. You can change how often it goes out under **Settings → Waitlist**.
</Info>
