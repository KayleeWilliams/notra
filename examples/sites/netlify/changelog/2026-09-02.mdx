- **Lane view** shows each block of pool time with its lanes side by side, so you can drag a class from lane 2 to lane 3
- Lanes take a depth and a water temperature, and Tidepool warns you when a beginner class lands in deep water
- Waitlisted parents get their position every two weeks by email or SMS

<Note>
  Lane view replaces the old **Rooms** tab. Your existing rooms became lanes automatically.
</Note>
