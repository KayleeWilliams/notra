- Parents get a **make-up credit** when they cancel before the cutoff or the instructor marks the swimmer absent
- Credits only offer open spots in the swimmer's own level and age group
- New **Settings → Make-ups** page for cutoff, expiry and credits per term
- Make-up swimmers show up on the instructor's roster with a small badge
