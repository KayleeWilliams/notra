---
title: "Live traffic re-routing"
date: 2026-10-02
version: "4.2.0"
description: "Routes re-plan themselves when traffic would make a stop late."
tags: [Feature]
image: /images/cover-traffic.png
---

- Active routes are checked against live traffic every five minutes
- Stops still ahead are re-ordered when one would miss its window by more than your tolerance
- **Settings → Routing → Live traffic** sets the tolerance, 10 minutes by default
- The driver app shows **Route updated** with the new next stop
