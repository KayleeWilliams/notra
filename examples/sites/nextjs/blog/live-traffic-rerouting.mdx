A route planned at 6 a.m. is a guess about the day. By 10 a.m. a closed bridge or a stalled truck on the ring road has made part of that guess wrong. Starting today, Pathway notices and fixes the rest of the route for you.

## What happens on the road

Every five minutes, Pathway checks each active route against live traffic. If the remaining stops still fit their time windows, nothing changes. If a stop would be late by more than your tolerance, Pathway re-orders the stops that are still ahead and sends the new order to the driver app.

The driver sees a short banner, **Route updated, 2 stops moved**, and the next stop on screen. They don't have to accept anything.

## What it never does

- It never moves a stop to another driver. Reassigning work stays a dispatcher decision.
- It never touches stops the driver has already started.
- It never re-plans more than twice an hour for the same route, so drivers aren't chasing a list that keeps changing.

<Note>
  Re-routing is on for new accounts. Existing accounts can turn it on under **Settings → Routing → Live traffic**, and set the lateness tolerance there. The default is 10 minutes.
</Note>

## Numbers from the pilot

Six fleets ran live re-routing for four weeks, 41,000 stops in total. On-time deliveries went from 88.4% to 94.1%, and drivers drove 3% fewer kilometres, mostly from avoiding the same jam twice.

## What's next

Dispatchers asked to see why a route changed. The next release adds a history panel on each route with every re-plan, the reason and the minutes it saved.
