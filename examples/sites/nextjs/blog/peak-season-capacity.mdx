Last December, stop volume across Pathway fleets peaked at 2.7 times the August average. The fleets that came through it on time didn't hire more drivers in a hurry. They knew in late summer how far their current routes could stretch.

## Three numbers to check now

| Number | Where to find it | Healthy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stops per route hour | **Reports → Routes** | Within 10% across depots |
| Slack per route | **Reports → Routes → Slack** | 30 to 60 minutes |
| Unplanned stops per day | **Reports → Planning** | Under 1% of volume |

Slack is the one people skip. A route with no slack in August has no room for the extra 20 stops it will get in December.

## Run a peak rehearsal

Pathway can plan a copy of any day with more volume than it had. Pick a normal Tuesday, scale it to 2.5×, and see how many routes and drivers the planner asks for.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Duplicate a day">
    Open **Planning**, pick the day and choose **Duplicate as scenario**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scale the stops">
    Set the multiplier. Pathway samples real addresses from your last 90 days, so the new stops land where your customers actually are.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare">
    The scenario shows the extra routes, the extra driver hours and the stops it couldn't fit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Scenarios never send anything to drivers or customers. Delete them when you're done, or keep them to compare against the real peak in January.
</Note>
