Until August, Cadence kept 1 in 10 `session_start` events for games with more than 50,000 daily players. It cut storage by a third. It also made funnels wrong in a way that was hard to spot.

## What went wrong

A funnel needs every step from the same player. With sampling, a player's `session_start` could be dropped while their `level_complete` was kept. The funnel then counted a finish without a start, and step two came out higher than step one.

Big games hid the error in the noise. A studio with 8,000 daily players saw a level that "converted" at 104%.

## What we do now

- Every event is stored. No sampling on ingest.
- Raw events older than 90 days are rolled up into daily counts per player.
- Funnels and retention read raw events for the last 90 days, and roll-ups before that.

## The cost

Storage went up 28%. We moved the raw tables to compressed columnar storage, which brought it back to 9% over the old bill. We're keeping that, and prices don't change.
