"Water every 7 days" is the most common advice on plant tags, and the most common way houseplants die. A monstera in a south window in July and the same monstera in a north room in November need very different amounts of water. Starting with Fernhill 3.4, the app plans each watering on its own.

## What goes into a watering date

For every plant, Fernhill now looks at four things:

- **Pot size and material.** A 12 cm plastic pot dries out more slowly than a 12 cm terracotta pot, and much faster than a 30 cm one.
- **Light.** The reading from the light meter, or the window direction you picked when you added the plant.
- **Season.** Days are shorter from October on, so plants drink less. The app stretches the gap between waterings by up to 60% in winter.
- **What you told it last time.** When you tap **Soil still damp**, the next gap gets longer. When you tap **Bone dry**, it gets shorter.

## Set it up for an existing plant

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the plant">
    Tap the plant on **My plants** and open **Care settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the pot">
    Pick the diameter and the material. If you're not sure, measure across the top rim.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Turn on adaptive watering">
    Switch **Watering** from **Fixed interval** to **Adaptive**. The next date moves right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  New plants use adaptive watering by default. Plants you added before 3.4 keep their fixed interval until you switch them, so nothing changes without you knowing.
</Note>

## What we saw in the beta

1,800 people used adaptive watering for six weeks. They got 31% fewer reminders than with fixed intervals, and the number of plants marked **Overwatered** in health checks dropped by half. Succulents and snake plants gained the most, because they were watered far too often before.
