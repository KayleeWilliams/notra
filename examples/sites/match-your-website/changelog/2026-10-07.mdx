- Monthly cloud-free mosaics for every land tile, rebuilt on the 1st
- New `source_date` band shows the capture day of each pixel
- Tiles above 5% cloud after 30 days are flagged `partial`
- Fixed footprints crossing the antimeridian returning an empty result

Read [how mosaics are built](/blog/cloud-free-mosaics).
