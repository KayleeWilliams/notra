---
title: "Jittered retries and per-endpoint rate limits"
date: 2026-09-16
version: "v3.1.0"
description: "Retries spread out after outages, and each endpoint gets a delivery rate limit."
tags: [Improvement]
---

- Retries use exponential backoff with full jitter, capped at 6 hours
- `rateLimit.requestsPerSecond` on endpoints, default 200
- Backlog size and drain time on the endpoint page

```typescript
await relaywave.endpoints.update("ep_7Hk2", {
  rateLimit: { requestsPerSecond: 50 },
});
```
