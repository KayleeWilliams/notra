date: 2026-10-07

description: "Public key webhook signatures for new endpoints."
tags: [Feature, Security]
---

- New endpoints sign deliveries with Ed25519 and send a `v2` signature
- **Endpoints → Signing → Switch to Ed25519** for existing endpoints, with 7 days of dual signatures
- `verifyWebhook` in `@relaywave/node` 4.0 accepts `publicKey`

```bash
bun add @relaywave/node@4.0.0
```

Read [why we switched](/blog/signing-webhooks-with-ed25519).
