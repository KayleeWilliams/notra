Most of our customers don't fill a 20-foot container. Until now, a few pallets out of Ningbo meant waiting for a forwarder to find enough cargo to share a box with. Starting this week, Harborline runs its own consolidated container from Ningbo to Rotterdam and Hamburg every week.

## How it works

You book by cubic meter, not by container. Your supplier delivers the goods to our partner warehouse in Beilun, we load every Thursday, and the vessel sails on Saturday.

| Step | Day | Who |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cargo cut-off at the Beilun warehouse | Tuesday, 17:00 local | Your supplier |
| Loading and seal | Thursday | Harborline |
| Vessel departs Ningbo | Saturday | Carrier |
| Arrival Rotterdam or Hamburg | About 32 days later | Carrier |

## What it costs

Prices are per cubic meter and include origin handling, ocean freight and destination unloading. Customs clearance and last-mile delivery are quoted separately, because they depend on what you import and where it goes.

<Note>
  The minimum booking is 1 m³. Anything under that is billed as 1 m³, which is still cheaper than air freight for most cartons over 40 kg.
</Note>

## Why weekly matters

A fixed sailing day means you can tell your supplier one date and plan stock around it. During the pilot in August and September, 38 customers shipped 214 m³ this way, and 9 out of 10 bookings made the Thursday load they were planned for.
