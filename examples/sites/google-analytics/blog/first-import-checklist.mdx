Most delays on a first shipment have nothing to do with the ship. They come from a missing number on a form, found two days after the container arrives. Here is what to sort out before your supplier hands the goods over.

## The checklist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get an EORI number">
    Every company that imports into the EU needs one. Apply with your national customs office. In Germany it takes about a week.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Agree on the Incoterm in writing">
    The Incoterm decides who pays for what and where the risk passes to you. Put it on the purchase order, not just in an email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the HS code for each product">
    The code sets your import duty. Harborline suggests one when you add a product, but check it with your broker for anything new.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Ask for the commercial invoice and packing list early">
    Both must show the same weights and carton counts. A mismatch is the most common reason customs holds a shipment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Book insurance">
    The carrier's liability is capped per kilogram, which rarely covers the value of what you bought.
  </Step>
</Steps>
