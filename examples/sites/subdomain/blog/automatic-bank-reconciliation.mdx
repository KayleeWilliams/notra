Reconciliation is the chore every founder puts off until the accountant asks for it. Starting today, Ledgerly does most of it for you.

## How matching works

Every night Ledgerly pulls new transactions from your bank and compares them against open invoices, bills and receipts. A match needs three things to line up:

- the amount, down to the cent, or within a fee you've told us about
- the counterparty name, after cleaning up bank noise like `SEPA DIRECT DEBIT REF 0042`
- a date within 10 days of the due date

When all three agree, the transaction is reconciled and the invoice is marked paid. When only two agree, it lands in **Review** with the suggested match pre-filled, so one click confirms it.

## What stays manual

Split payments and transactions in a different currency than the invoice still go to **Review**. We'd rather ask than guess with your books.

<Note>
  Matching runs on new transactions only. To match older ones, open **Bank → Transactions**, select them and click **Match now**.
</Note>

## Numbers from the beta

Across 240 beta companies, 91% of transactions matched automatically in the first month, and the average close went from 6 days to 2.
