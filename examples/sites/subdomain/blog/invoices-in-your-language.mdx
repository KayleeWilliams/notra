Half of Ledgerly's customers sell across borders, and their invoices should read naturally on the other side.

## Set it once per customer

Open a customer, pick **Invoice language**, and every invoice, reminder and credit note for them uses it. Dates, number formats and tax labels follow the language too, so a German invoice says `USt.` and uses `1.234,56 €`.
