You don't need a finance team to close your books every month. You need a checklist and an hour.

## The checklist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Reconcile every bank account">
    Every transaction should be matched to an invoice, bill or receipt.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Chase missing receipts">
    Ledgerly lists card payments without a receipt under **Expenses → Missing**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review open invoices">
    Send a reminder for anything more than 14 days overdue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Book recurring costs">
    Rent, payroll and subscriptions, so the month shows what it really cost.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look at the numbers">
    Compare revenue and burn against last month, and write down one sentence about why they changed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Why monthly

Closing once a year means reconstructing twelve months from memory. Closing monthly means remembering last week.
