My garage bench was a door on two sawhorses. It moved every time I planed anything. This is the replacement, built from twelve 2×4s that cost less than a good chisel.

## Cut list

| Part | Count | Size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Top laminations | 8 | 1.8 m |
| Legs | 4 | 85 cm, doubled |
| Stretchers | 4 | 1.4 m |

## What I'd tell my past self

<Steps>
  <Step title="Let the wood sit for a week">
    Construction lumber arrives wet. Mine moved almost 3 mm across the width after I flattened it the first time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Glue the top in sections">
    Four boards at a time. Gluing all eight at once needed more clamps and more hands than I had.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Drill the vise holes before assembly">
    I didn't, and boring a 25 mm hole through a finished leg while lying on the floor was not fun.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The bench weighs about 70 kg now. It does not move.
