Every sharpening forum thread ends with someone recommending a 300-euro set of waterstones. I use three things:

- a 600-grit diamond plate, for the bevel
- a leather strop with green compound, for the edge
- a side-clamp honing guide that cost 14 euros

## The routine

I hone a 30° micro-bevel on the plate, about ten strokes, then strop until the burr is gone. It takes two minutes per chisel. When the micro-bevel gets wide, I regrind the main bevel at 25° on the plate. That happens maybe twice a year.

## The test

Shave end grain on a piece of pine. If it leaves a shiny surface, the chisel is sharp. If it crushes the fibers, go back to the plate.
