## Switching plans

<Check>Monthly subscribers can switch to yearly from **Billing** at any time. We credit the unused days of your current month.</Check>

<Warning title="Prices for existing teams">
  Teams that signed up before September 15 keep $29 per month until September 2027. The new price applies after that.
</Warning>

<Danger>
  Cancelling a yearly plan stops renewal but doesn't refund the current year. Write to us in the first 14 days if you change your mind.
</Danger>

## Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I keep using icons after I cancel?" defaultOpen>

  <Accordion title="Do you offer discounts for students or nonprofits?">

  <Accordion title="Is there a lifetime plan?">
