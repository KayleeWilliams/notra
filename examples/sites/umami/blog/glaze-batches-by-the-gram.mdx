Most studios know how many bags of clay they have. Very few know how much Gerstley borate is left until someone scoops the bottom of the tub. Glaze materials are where inventory quietly drifts.

## Mix a batch, update the shelf

Open a glaze recipe, enter the batch size and click **Mix batch**. Kilnworks scales the recipe and subtracts each material from stock. A 5,000 g batch of a classic celadon looks like this:

| Material | Recipe % | Batch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Custer feldspar | 25 | 1,250 g |
| Silica 325 | 35 | 1,750 g |
| Whiting | 20 | 1,000 g |
| EPK kaolin | 20 | 1,000 g |
| Red iron oxide (add) | 1 | 50 g |

Additions like colorants and opacifiers are calculated on top of the 100%, the way potters already write them.

## Substitutions are logged

Ran out of Custer and used G-200 instead? Swap the material in the batch dialog. The batch record keeps both the recipe and what actually went into the bucket, so when a glaze crawls three firings later you can see why.

<Note>
  Batches mixed before this release aren't deducted. Run a shelf count under **Inventory → Count** once, and Kilnworks tracks from there.
</Note>

## What we saw in the beta

Across 38 studios, the gap between counted and expected stock for glaze materials dropped from 14% to under 3% in the first month. The biggest win was fewer emergency orders of silica.
